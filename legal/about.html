About workwimme

workwimme publishes independent, plain-English guides to finding and applying for jobs at well-known employers. We point readers to official employer career sites wherever possible.

We are not a recruiter or staffing agency, we do not offer jobs, and we are not affiliated with or endorsed by the employers we cover. Some pages contain sponsored links to third-party job search platforms; see our disclosure.
