Types of Amazon hourly jobs

Amazon's hourly hiring site, hiring.amazon.com, lists roles such as fulfillment center and delivery station warehouse associates, with full-time and part-time options[1]. Customer service and retail roles have their own hiring processes[3].

Pay: pay varies by role and location. Check the individual posting on the official careers site.

Amazon benefits

Amazon says its benefits can include health care starting on day one, employee discounts, 401(k) savings plans and paid time off[1]. Offerings vary by work location, scheduled hours, length of employment and job type (for example regular vs seasonal)[2].

Amazon hiring age

Amazon warehouse postings state candidates must be 18 or older, with the ability to read and speak English for safety purposes[1]. Check each posting for its requirements.

How to apply at Amazon

  1. Search roles on hiring.amazon.com and choose a job, location and shift.
  2. Complete the online application. Amazon says no resume is needed for hourly warehouse roles and the application usually takes about twenty minutes[3].
  3. Attend the in-person pre-hire appointment and bring proper I-9 identification[3].

Tips

  • Apply only on Amazon's official hiring site; never pay to apply.
  • Read each posting's safety and schedule requirements.
  • Bring the documents listed on your pre-hire checklist.

Amazon jobs FAQ

How do I apply for Amazon jobs?

Use Amazon's official hourly hiring site, hiring.amazon.com: pick a role, location and shift, then complete the online application.

Do I need a resume for Amazon warehouse jobs?

Amazon says hourly warehouse roles do not require a resume.

What is the Amazon hiring age?

Amazon warehouse postings state candidates must be 18 or older.

How much do Amazon jobs pay?

Pay varies by role and location. Check the individual posting on hiring.amazon.com.
