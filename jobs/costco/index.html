Types of Costco jobs

Costco's careers site groups its opportunities into several areas[1]:

  • Warehouses and Business Centers: in-store roles in a fast-paced environment, such as cashier, front end, stocking, and departments like optical.
  • Distribution (depots and logistics): roles supporting how merchandise moves to warehouses.
  • Member Service Centers: employees who respond to member phone calls, emails and chats.
  • Home and regional offices: Costco says it fills most merchandising and corporate positions by promoting employees from its warehouses, and recruits externally for specialized areas such as IT, ecommerce and accounting.

Costco notes that the job types on its site are examples of positions it may hire for when openings exist, not a guarantee that a position is open[1].

Costco employee benefits

Costco says it offers benefits to eligible full-time and part-time employees[2], including:

  • Medical, pharmacy, dental and vision coverage, with eligibility for spouses, children and domestic partners
  • 401(k) plan and employee stock purchase plan
  • Paid sick and vacation time, eight paid holidays and one floating holiday
  • Life, AD&D and disability insurance options
  • Programs such as a College Student Retention Program and paid bonding leave

Costco also states it guarantees minimum scheduled hours for full-time and part-time employees and posts weekly schedules at least three weeks in advance[2]. Eligibility rules apply. Confirm details with Costco.

Pay: pay varies by role and location. Check the individual posting on Costco's official careers site.

Costco hiring age

Costco's official careers site does not state one minimum hiring age. Several widely cited sources report that the minimum age to work at Costco in the U.S. is 18[4]. Because requirements can depend on the role and local law, check the requirements in each posting.

How to apply at Costco

  1. Go to careers.costco.com, Costco's official careers site[3].
  2. Search by career area or location to find Costco jobs near you. For warehouse jobs, Costco says to apply directly to the location you want[1].
  3. Submit the online application for the role.
  4. Follow up with the location. Costco says application updates are not sent by email or messaging apps and recommends contacting the location directly[3].

Having technical trouble with the online application? Costco's FAQ lists its Human Resources line for help[3].

Costco jobs FAQ

How do I apply for Costco jobs?

Costco directs U.S. applicants to its official careers site, careers.costco.com, where you can search openings and submit an online application. For warehouse jobs, Costco says to apply directly to the location you are interested in.

How do I find Costco jobs near me?

Use the location search on Costco's official careers site, which lists warehouses, distribution and member service center roles by location. Costco notes it does not currently operate in Wyoming, West Virginia or Rhode Island.

What is the Costco hiring age?

Costco's careers site does not publish a single minimum hiring age. Several widely cited sources report a minimum of 18 in the U.S., but requirements can vary by role and location, so check the specific job posting.

How do I check my Costco application status?

According to Costco's customer service FAQ, application updates are not sent by email or messaging apps; it recommends contacting the location you applied to directly.

How much do Costco jobs pay?

Pay varies by role and location. Check individual postings on Costco's official careers site for current details.

Are the people who hand out samples Costco employees?

No. Costco states that in-warehouse product sampling is handled by Club Demonstration Services (CDS), a separate company with its own hiring.
