Fast Food Jobs Hiring Immediately: How to Get Hired Faster

Need a job soon? Fast food is one of the quickest industries to get started in: openings come up often and most roles are taught on the job. This guide explains what "hiring immediately" really means, how to apply at Dunkin' and Domino's, and the practical steps that can shorten the time between applying and your first shift.

What "hiring immediately" really means

When a listing says "hiring immediately" or "immediate start," it usually means the restaurant has an open shift to fill and would like the new person to start soon. It is not a promise that you'll be hired, and it doesn't skip the normal steps. You'll still apply, usually talk with a manager, and complete paperwork. Some roles, like delivery driving, also add checks such as a review of your driving record.

So the realistic goal is not "a job today," but "an offer as quickly as the restaurant's process allows." That part you can influence.

Why fast food restaurants hire often

The numbers explain why fast food is a common first stop for people who need work soon. The U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics (BLS) counts about 3.9 million fast food and counter workers and projects about 1,078,500 openings each year, on average, across food and beverage serving jobs over the 2025–35 decade, mostly from people moving on to other work[1].

BLS also notes that these jobs typically need no formal education or work experience, training is short-term and on the job, and part-time work is common[1]. In other words, restaurants regularly need people and are set up to train newcomers, which tends to make hiring faster than in jobs that require credentials. Whether a specific location near you is hiring depends on the day.

Dunkin': how to apply

Dunkin' directs job seekers to its official careers site, careers.dunkindonuts.com, where you can search jobs by location[2]. The site also features a recruitment chat assistant that says it can answer questions, help you find a job and help you apply, and you can join a Talent Community to be notified when suitable roles open[2].

Most Dunkin' restaurants are run by independent franchisees. Dunkin' notes that franchisees handle all employment matters in their restaurants, including benefits and perks, so details vary by location[2]. Typical restaurant roles include crew member and shift leader. Our Dunkin' jobs guide has more on how franchise hiring works.

Domino's: how to apply

Domino's lists store openings at both corporate and franchise locations on jobs.dominos.com, including delivery driver and pizza maker roles[3]. For franchise stores, Domino's postings say the franchisee alone makes hiring, pay and scheduling decisions[4]. Driver postings we reviewed require drivers to be at least 18 with a valid license and a reliable insured vehicle, and to pass background and motor vehicle record (MVR) checks[4], so in-store roles may be quicker if you don't have a car. How to get hired faster

You can't control a restaurant's timeline, but these steps remove the most common delays:

  1. Apply to several locations the same day. Franchise restaurants hire independently, so applying at three or four nearby stores multiplies your chances.
  2. Make your availability obvious. Early mornings, evenings, weekends and holidays are often the hardest shifts to fill. If you can work them, say so clearly.
  3. Keep your phone on and check messages. Managers often reach out by text or call. Replying within hours, not days, keeps you at the top of the list.
  4. Have your documents ready. You'll need identification to prove you can work in the U.S. when you're hired, plus your driver's license and insurance for driving roles.
  5. Line up a reference or two. A teacher, coach, or former supervisor who can vouch for your reliability.
  6. Be flexible on role. If the position you wanted is filled, ask what else is open.

After you apply

If you haven't heard back in a few days, follow up. A short, polite phone call or a visit during a quiet time, such as mid-afternoon rather than lunch or dinner rush, shows you're serious. Ask for the manager, give your name and the date you applied, and restate your availability.

In the interview, expect practical questions: when you can work, how you'd handle a busy rush or an unhappy customer, and whether you're comfortable on your feet for a whole shift. Keep answers short and honest. If you get an offer, confirm your start date, schedule, uniform and what to bring on day one.

Under 18? Child labor rules limit the hours and tasks for younger workers, and each employer sets its own minimum age for each role. Check the posting, and see the U.S. Department of Labor's YouthRules! site for federal rules[5].

Red flags to watch for

  • Requests for money. The FTC says honest employers will never ask you to pay to get a job, and warns that scammers often charge for so-called training or certifications[6].
  • Offers without an application or interview, especially through social media messages.
  • Requests to buy gift cards or to deposit a check and send money back. The FTC describes these as common signs of a fake job[6].

Fast food jobs hiring immediately FAQ

Which fast food jobs are hiring immediately?

No one can promise that a particular restaurant will hire you right away. Fast food restaurants do hire often, because the industry has a large number of openings every year and most roles are learned on the job. To find current openings, search by location on official careers sites such as careers.dunkindonuts.com and jobs.dominos.com, or on a job search site.

How fast can you get hired at a fast food restaurant?

It depends on the restaurant, the role and what checks are required. Some locations move quickly from application to interview, while others take longer, especially if a background or driving-record check is needed. Applying with clear availability and responding quickly to messages can help keep things moving.

Do I need experience to work in fast food?

Usually not. The U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics says food and beverage serving workers typically need no formal education or work experience and learn their skills on the job.

How do I apply for Dunkin' jobs?

Search and apply on Dunkin's official careers site, careers.dunkindonuts.com, which also has a recruitment chat assistant that can help you find and apply for jobs. At franchise locations, the independent franchisee handles hiring.

How old do you have to be to work at a fast food restaurant?

It depends on the employer, the role and federal and state child labor rules. Domino's delivery driver postings we reviewed require drivers to be at least 18. Check the requirements in each posting.

How much do fast food jobs pay?

Pay varies by employer, role and location. Franchise owners set their own wages, so check the individual listing.
