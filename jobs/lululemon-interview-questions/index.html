How the Lululemon interview process works

You apply for a specific store and role on Lululemon's careers site[5]. Lululemon's postings say only applicants under consideration will be contacted[1]. Lululemon doesn't publish a fixed interview format for store roles, but Educator candidates on Glassdoor and Indeed (candidate-reported) commonly describe[2][3]:

  1. A group interview, often over video, with a few candidates and one or more store leaders.
  2. A one-on-one interview with a store leader, often in the store.
  3. An offer if the store moves forward, sometimes after a short wait.

Leadership roles may add more rounds. Treat these as typical reports, not a fixed format; your store may run things differently.

What Lululemon looks for

Lululemon's Educator postings list the key skills and core values it wants[1], and they're a good map for your answers:

  • Inclusion and diversity: creating an environment that values differences.
  • Integrity and honesty.
  • Guest experience: enjoying connecting with and helping guests.
  • Personal responsibility: being accountable and dependable.
  • Self-awareness: knowing how your words and actions affect others.
  • Collaboration and teamwork.
  • Enthusiasm: finding ways to make work fun and engaging.

The same postings describe the Educator as an expert in guest experience who shares product education and speaks authentically about community and culture[1]. See our Lululemon jobs guide for the full role and requirements.

Common Lululemon interview questions (and how to answer)

These are question types candidates commonly report on Glassdoor, Indeed and Reddit[2][3][4], plus questions that follow from the values and duties in the postings.

"What's a goal you're working toward, and how are you getting there?"

Candidates say goal questions come up often. Pick a real, specific goal (a race, a skill, a class) and describe the steps you're taking and how you track progress. Specific beats impressive.

"Tell me about a time you dealt with a challenging customer."

Show you listened, stayed calm and found a solution. Retail, food service, school projects or volunteering all count.

"A guest is upset because the item they want isn't in stock. What do you do?"

Acknowledge the frustration, then offer options: a similar product, another size or color, checking another store or ordering online for pickup. The postings mention omnichannel options such as buy online, pick up in store[1], so it's fair to mention them.

"Tell me about a time you noticed someone being left out."

This maps to inclusion. Describe what you noticed, what you did to include the person and what changed.

"How do you handle several tasks or deadlines at once?"

Explain how you prioritize. Stores juggle guests, restocking and online orders, so a simple system (lists, check-ins with a lead) is a good answer.

"Tell me about something you're passionate about."

The postings value enthusiasm and product education[1], so be ready to talk about an activity you love. It doesn't have to be yoga or running. Explain it as if you were teaching it, since Educators teach guests about products.

"Why Lululemon?"

Be specific. Mention the guest experience and community focus in the role, and how it fits your goals.

How to stand out in the Lululemon group interview

Candidates often describe the group interview as a test of how you treat other people as much as how you answer[4]. Tips candidates and former team members share:

  • Listen actively. Nod, make eye contact (or camera contact) and don't plan your answer while someone else is talking.
  • Build on others' answers. A short "I liked what you said about..." shows collaboration.
  • Take turns. You don't have to answer first. If two people start at once, offer to let the other person go.
  • Ask a question at the end. Ask about the team, training or what makes someone successful in the role.

On video, test your connection, find a quiet spot and keep your camera at eye level.

How to answer: the STAR-L method

Former team members on Reddit recommend structuring behavioral answers with STAR-L[4] (candidate-reported, not an official rule):

  • Situation: set the scene briefly.
  • Task: what you were responsible for.
  • Action: what you did.
  • Result: what happened.
  • Learning: what you learned or would do differently.

Prepare three or four stories you can adapt: one about a customer, one about teamwork, one about a goal and one about feedback or a mistake. Our Sephora interview questions guide uses a similar approach for beauty retail.

What to wear and bring

Lululemon doesn't publish an interview dress code. Wear something neat and comfortable that you feel confident in, and ask the person who scheduled your interview if you're unsure. Bring your availability (postings ask for evenings, weekends and holidays[1]), a couple of questions for the interviewer and, for in-person interviews, a copy of your resume.

After the interview

  • Follow up once with a short thank-you if you have the interviewer's contact.
  • Keep applying. Postings say only applicants under consideration will be contacted[1], so don't wait on one store.
  • Watch for scams. Real offers come through official channels. The FTC says honest employers will never ask you to pay to get a job[6].

Lululemon interview FAQ

What questions does Lululemon ask in an interview?

Candidates commonly report behavioral and situational questions: a personal goal you're working toward, a time you dealt with a challenging customer, how you'd help a guest when an item is out of stock, a time you noticed someone being left out, and how you manage several tasks or deadlines. These are candidate reports, not an official list.

What is the Lululemon group interview like?

Many Educator candidates report a group interview first, often over video, with several candidates answering questions with a store leader. Candidates say listening, acknowledging other people's answers and taking turns matter as much as your own answers. A one-on-one interview often follows. Formats vary by store.

What is the STAR-L method for Lululemon interviews?

Candidates and former team members often recommend answering with STAR-L: Situation, Task, Action, Result and Learning, meaning what you learned or would do differently. It's a candidate-reported tip, not an official Lululemon rule, but it's a useful structure for any behavioral question.

What should I wear to a Lululemon interview?

Lululemon doesn't publish an interview dress code. Wear something neat and comfortable that you feel confident in. If you're unsure, ask the person who scheduled your interview.

How long does Lululemon take to hire?

It varies by store and season. Lululemon's postings say only applicants under consideration will be contacted, so if you haven't heard back, it's fine to keep applying to other openings.

How old do you have to be to work at Lululemon?

The U.S. Educator postings we reviewed say applicants must be 18 or older. Check the eligibility section of the posting you apply to.
