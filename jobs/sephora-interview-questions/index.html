How the Sephora interview process usually works

Sephora posts U.S. store roles on its official careers site, jobs.sephora.com, with listings for specific stores and schedule types[1]. Sephora doesn't publish a fixed interview process, but candidate reports describe a few common patterns:

  • Phone or video screen. A short call to confirm your availability and interest.
  • In-store interview. On Indeed, an on-site interview is the step candidates mention most often, followed by a phone screen[4].
  • Group interview or hiring event. Some candidates report group interviews. Sephora has also run retail hiring weeks with on-the-spot interviews at designated times[5].

The process depends on the store, the role and the time of year. For the application itself, see our Sephora jobs guide.

Sephora at Kohl's is different. Sephora at Kohl's roles are posted on Kohl's careers site, so the application and interview process follows Kohl's[6].

What Sephora looks for

The best guide to Sephora interview questions is the job posting. The Beauty Advisor postings we reviewed list these responsibilities and qualifications[1][2]:

  • Personalized client service: welcoming clients, giving consultations and recommending products that fit their needs.
  • Product knowledge and trends: keeping up with new products and techniques, and sharing that knowledge.
  • Results: working toward sales goals and key performance indicators.
  • A welcoming store: supporting inventory and merchandising and keeping the space clean and organized.
  • Inclusion and teamwork: helping create a culture where everyone feels welcome.
  • Experience: previous experience giving excellent service and building client relationships in a retail, hospitality or other client-focused environment.
  • Practical requirements: availability during peak retail times, including nights, weekends and holidays, and the ability to lift and carry up to 50 pounds.

Almost every interview question will connect to one of these points, so prepare a short example for each.

Common Sephora interview questions (and how to answer)

These are question types candidates commonly report for Beauty Advisor and store roles. They are not an official Sephora list.

  1. "Why do you want to work at Sephora?" Go beyond "I love makeup." Mention how you like helping people find products that suit them, and something specific you've noticed in the store.
  2. "Tell me about your customer service experience." Pick one example that shows how you made someone feel welcome and solved their problem. Hospitality, food service and volunteer roles count.
  3. "How would you help a client who doesn't know what they want?" Describe asking open questions (skin type, routine, budget, occasion), narrowing options and letting them try before they buy.
  4. "Tell me about a time you handled a difficult or unhappy customer." Show that you listened, stayed calm and found a fair solution.
  5. "How do you keep up with beauty trends?" Name real sources you use and a recent trend or product you've tried. Be honest about your strongest category, whether that's skincare, makeup, fragrance or hair.
  6. "How do you make every client feel welcome?" Inclusion is part of the role. Talk about greeting everyone, not making assumptions, and being comfortable with every shade and skin type.
  7. "How do you feel about sales goals?" Explain that you see recommendations as service and that you track how you're doing. Mention any targets you've worked toward.
  8. "Describe a time you worked with a team to get through a busy period." Holiday rushes and launches are busy. Pick an example where you helped coworkers.
  9. "Tell me about a time you learned something new quickly." Postings ask for a quick learner, so show how you pick up product or process knowledge.
  10. "What is your availability?" Be specific and honest about nights, weekends and holidays.
  11. "What's your favorite product right now, and how would you sell it?" This often leads into a role-play (below).

Use the Situation, Task, Action, Result structure for story questions, and keep each answer to about a minute.

The product recommendation role-play

Many candidates report being asked to "sell" a product or help a pretend client. Interviewers usually care more about how you ask questions than about product facts. A simple flow:

  1. Greet and ask. "What brings you in today?" Then ask about their routine, concerns and what they've tried.
  2. Recommend one or two options and explain why each fits what they told you.
  3. Offer to let them try it or show how to apply it.
  4. Mention the next step, such as a complementary product or Sephora's Beauty Insider loyalty program[3], without being pushy.
  5. Thank them and invite them back.

Group interview tips

  • Include others. Invite quieter candidates into the conversation. Interviewers notice teamwork.
  • Listen first. Build on what others say instead of repeating it.
  • Stay positive and keep answers short so everyone gets time.
  • Be yourself. Rehearsed lines tend to sound flat in a group setting.

What to wear to a Sephora interview

Sephora doesn't publish an interview dress code. Among Indeed users who described their Beauty Advisor interview, business casual was the most common answer[4]. A polished, comfortable outfit that suits you is a safe choice. Makeup is a personal choice. If you wear it, keep it intentional and be ready to explain which products you used and why.

Questions to ask the interviewer

  • "What does training look like for new Beauty Advisors?"
  • "How does the team share product knowledge?"
  • "What does a strong first three months look like in this role?"
  • "What are the next steps after today?"

After the interview

Send a short thank-you message if you have the interviewer's contact details, and watch your email for next steps. If you don't hear back, a single polite follow-up with the store is reasonable. Never pay anyone to get an interview or a job. The FTC says honest employers will never ask you to pay to get a job[7].

Sephora interview FAQ

What questions does Sephora ask in a Beauty Advisor interview?

Sephora does not publish an interview question list, and questions vary by store. Candidates commonly report questions about customer service experience, handling difficult clients, teamwork, availability, why you want to work at Sephora, and role-play scenarios such as recommending a product.

Does Sephora do group interviews?

Some candidates report group interviews, especially at busy hiring times, while others report a phone screen followed by a one-on-one interview in the store. The format depends on the store and the season.

What should I wear to a Sephora interview?

Sephora does not publish an interview dress code. Candidates on Indeed most often describe business casual. Choose a polished, comfortable outfit; makeup is a personal choice, and if you wear it, be ready to talk about the products you used.

Do I need makeup artistry experience to work at Sephora?

Not necessarily. Sephora Beauty Advisor postings ask for experience giving excellent service and building client relationships in retail, hospitality or another client-focused setting, plus a passion for beauty and the ability to learn product knowledge.

What availability does Sephora ask for?

Sephora Beauty Advisor postings list full-time (30 to 40 hours a week), part-time (15 to 29 hours) and flex (4 to 14 hours, as needed) schedules, and ask for availability during peak retail times, including nights, weekends and holidays.

Is Sephora at Kohl's the same application?

No. Sephora at Kohl's roles are posted on Kohl's own careers site, so apply there if you want a Sephora at Kohl's position.
