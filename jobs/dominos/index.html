How Domino's hiring works

Many Domino's stores are owned and operated by independent franchisees. Domino's postings for those stores state the franchisee alone makes hiring, pay, benefits and scheduling decisions and would be your only employer[1].

Types of Domino's jobs

Domino's careers site, jobs.dominos.com, lists roles including delivery drivers, in full-time and part-time positions[1]. Domino's notes many team members started as drivers or pizza makers[1].

Pay: pay varies by role and location. Check the individual posting on the official careers site.

Delivery driver requirements and hiring age

Domino's delivery driver postings we reviewed require drivers to be at least 18, with a valid driver's license, a reliable insured vehicle and a safe driving record; some also require a minimum driving history and a background/MVR check[1][2]. Requirements vary by store.

How to apply at Domino's

  1. Search openings on jobs.dominos.com by location.
  2. Open the posting for the store and role you want and review its requirements.
  3. Apply online through the posting.

Tips

  • For driver roles, have your license, registration and insurance details ready.
  • Apply only through official listings; never pay to apply.
  • Wondering what happens after you apply? See our Domino's hiring process guide and Domino's interview questions.
