Part-Time Jobs Near Me: Employers, Schedules and How to Apply

Looking for part-time jobs near you? Whether you're fitting work around school, caregiving or another job, the right part-time role depends as much on the schedule as on the job itself. What counts as part-time?

There's no universal rule. For its labor statistics, the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics (BLS) classifies people who usually work fewer than 35 hours a week as part-time, and is clear that this is a statistical definition, not a legal one[1]. In practice, each employer decides how many hours a part-time role includes, and that can range from a couple of shifts a week to close to full time.

That's why the label on a listing matters less than the details. Two "part-time" jobs can look very different once you know the typical weekly hours, how far ahead schedules are posted, and whether the hours are guaranteed.

Costco says it offers healthcare coverage and benefits to full-time and part-time employees, guarantees minimum scheduled hours for both, and posts weekly schedules at least three weeks in advance[2]. Eligibility rules apply. See our Costco jobs guide and Costco shifts and part-time hours.

Amazon's benefits page groups regular hourly schedules into full and reduced time (30–40 hours a week) and part time (20–29 hours), and says benefits vary by work location, scheduled hours, length of employment and job type[4]. Questions to ask before you accept

  • How many hours a week do part-time employees usually get? Ask for a typical range, not just the minimum.
  • Are hours guaranteed? Some employers guarantee minimum hours; others, like flex roles, don't.
  • How far in advance are schedules posted? This matters if you're balancing classes, childcare or another job.
  • Am I eligible for benefits? Eligibility often depends on hours worked and length of employment.
  • What's expected around holidays and peak seasons? Retail and restaurants may need more availability at busy times.
  • Can I move to full time later? Useful if your needs might change.

How to apply for part-time jobs

  1. Decide your real availability: the days and hours you can reliably work every week.
  2. Search by location on official careers sites or a job search site, and filter for part-time listings.
  3. Read the job type in each posting: part-time, flex or seasonal can mean very different schedules.
  4. Apply to several employers so you can compare schedules and pick the best fit.
  5. State your availability clearly in the application and interview. Being upfront avoids schedule conflicts later.

Tips for landing a part-time job

  • Offer the shifts that are hardest to fill. Weekends, evenings and early mornings often make you a stronger candidate.
  • New to work? See our guide to retail jobs with no experience for application tips.
  • Want maximum flexibility? Compare part-time employment with app-based work in our gig jobs guide.
  • Never pay to apply. The FTC says honest employers will never ask you to pay to get a job[10].

Part-time jobs FAQ

How many hours is a part-time job?

There is no single legal definition. For its statistics, the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics counts people who usually work fewer than 35 hours a week as part-time, but notes this is not a legal definition. Each employer sets its own hours for part-time roles, so check the posting.

How do I find part-time jobs near me?

Search by city or ZIP code on employer careers sites and filter by job type, or use a job search site that lets you filter for part-time listings. Employers such as Costco, Amazon, Sephora, Dunkin' and Domino's list part-time options at many locations.

Do part-time jobs offer benefits?

Some do, depending on the employer and eligibility rules. Costco says it offers benefits to eligible full-time and part-time employees, and Amazon says its benefits vary by location, scheduled hours, length of employment and job type. Confirm details in the posting.

Are part-time hours guaranteed?

It depends on the employer. Costco says it guarantees minimum scheduled hours for full-time and part-time employees, while Sephora lists its flex positions with non-guaranteed hours. Ask about typical weekly hours before you accept.

Can I work part-time while in school or at another job?

Often, yes. Many retail and restaurant employers schedule evenings and weekends, and BLS notes part-time work is common in food service. Share your availability clearly when you apply.

How much do part-time jobs pay?

Pay varies by employer, role and location. Check the individual listing for current details.
