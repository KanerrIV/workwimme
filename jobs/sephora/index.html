Types of Sephora store jobs

Sephora's official careers site, jobs.sephora.com, lists store roles such as Beauty Advisor in full-time, part-time and flex positions[1]. Beauty Advisors welcome clients and offer personalized consultations and product recommendations[1]. Flex positions are listed with non-guaranteed hours[2].

Pay: pay varies by role and location. Check the individual posting on the official careers site.

Sephora benefits

Sephora job postings say it offers comprehensive healthcare and wellbeing benefits based on eligibility[1]. Eligibility rules apply; confirm details in the posting.

Sephora hiring age

Sephora's postings we reviewed do not state one minimum hiring age. Check the requirements in each posting.

How to apply at Sephora

  1. Search openings on jobs.sephora.com by location and role.
  2. Review the posting (store, schedule type, requirements).
  3. Submit the online application for that store.

Tips

  • Note whether a role is full-time, part-time or flex.
  • Highlight customer service experience and genuine interest in beauty.
  • Apply only on the official careers site; never pay to apply.

Sephora jobs FAQ

How do I apply for Sephora jobs?

Search and apply on Sephora's official careers site, jobs.sephora.com.

Does Sephora hire part-time?

Sephora lists Beauty Advisor roles as full-time, part-time and flex, depending on the store.

How much do Sephora jobs pay?

Pay varies by role and location. Sephora lists pay ranges on its job postings.
