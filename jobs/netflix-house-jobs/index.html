Netflix House Jobs: Crew Roles, Hiring Age and How to Apply

Netflix House is Netflix's permanent, year-round fan venue, and it hires hourly crew for ticketing, attractions, retail and events. For people outside tech and entertainment production, these are likely the most accessible in-person jobs at Netflix. This guide covers where Netflix House is hiring, what Crew Members do, the hiring age and requirements, and how to apply through Netflix House's official careers site.

What Netflix House is

Netflix describes Netflix House as a permanent entertainment venue where fans interact with Netflix stories through immersive experiences and hospitality[6]. Its postings describe a "first-of-its-kind, permanent, year-round home for fans" that's free to enter, with a mix of free and paid experiences[3]. That means the jobs look a lot like theme park, attraction and retail work, not office work.

Where Netflix House is hiring

Netflix House's careers site lists these locations[1]:

  • Philadelphia: Netflix House Philadelphia, in the King of Prussia mall, Pennsylvania.
  • Dallas: Netflix House Dallas, in the Galleria Dallas mall, Texas.
  • Las Vegas: planned to open on the Las Vegas Strip in 2027.

Netflix House says it's exploring more locations[1]. If you don't live near one of these venues, look for similar guest-service work in retail, attractions or events near you.

Netflix House roles

Netflix House says it has positions across retail, sales, attractions, programming, ticketing and events, split into Crew Member and Management teams[2]. Hourly crew roles in the postings we reviewed include:

  • House Crew Member: greeting, ticketing, retail operations, fan service, mini-golf, VR games, photo operations, TUDUM Theater operations and events. Crew Members also sell and scan tickets and manage guest flow and queues[3].
  • Retail Crew Member: sales floor coverage, cashiering, merchandising, stockroom, receiving and inventory counts[4].
  • Crew Lead and events roles: lead and supervisor positions also appear on the careers site, along with show and performance roles.

Postings also stress acting as a "Netflix ambassador" who's passionate about talking with fans about Netflix series and films[3].

Netflix House hiring age and requirements

The crew postings we reviewed say applicants must be at least 18 years of age to be considered[3][4]. They also ask for:

  • Experience in fast-paced retail, hospitality, attractions or food and beverage operations.
  • Knowledge of the Netflix platform and shows.
  • Strong teamwork, customer service, conflict resolution and communication skills.
  • Willingness to learn environmental, health and safety protocols.
  • For retail roles, comfort with point-of-sale systems and the ability to lift stock, typically up to 50 pounds[4].

Fluency in additional languages is listed as a plus[3].

Shifts and schedules

Netflix House says it offers both fixed and shift-based opportunities[2]. The postings we reviewed describe on-site hourly roles: the King of Prussia House Crew Member role is part-time[3], while a Dallas Crew Member posting and the King of Prussia Retail Crew Member posting are full-time or part-time[4][5]. Postings ask for availability to work a flexible schedule, including weekends and holidays, "at a 365-day operation"[3].

Pay: pay varies by role and location. Check the individual posting for current details.

How to apply for Netflix House jobs

  1. Start at the official site. Go to jobs.netflixhouse.com and choose a location[1].
  2. Read the posting. Check the age, schedule and experience requirements.
  3. Apply soon. Postings say they're open for at least 7 days and are removed when the position is filled[3].
  4. Ask for accommodations if needed. Netflix House says to send accommodation requests to your recruiting partner[1].

Tips for getting hired

  • Show guest-service experience. Retail, restaurant, theme park, cinema and event jobs are all relevant.
  • Know the shows. Postings ask for knowledge of Netflix shows, so be ready to talk about titles you love.
  • Be clear on availability. Weekends and holidays are part of a 365-day operation.
  • Watch for fake recruiters. Netflix says it will never ask you to share personal information such as bank details or passwords in a text or email, and it asks you to forward suspicious emails and texts to phishing@netflix.com[7]. Our Netflix remote jobs guide covers common Netflix job scams.

Netflix House jobs FAQ

Where are Netflix House jobs?

Netflix House's careers site lists two open venues: Netflix House Philadelphia in the King of Prussia mall in Pennsylvania, and Netflix House Dallas in the Galleria Dallas mall in Texas. A third location is planned for the Las Vegas Strip in 2027.

How old do you have to be to work at Netflix House?

The Netflix House crew postings we reviewed say applicants must be at least 18 years of age to be considered for employment.

What does a Netflix House Crew Member do?

Crew Members work across greeting, ticketing, retail, fan service, mini-golf, VR games, photo operations, theater operations and events, according to Netflix House's postings. Retail Crew Members also handle cashiering, merchandising, stockroom and inventory work.

Are Netflix House jobs part-time?

Netflix House says it offers both fixed and shift-based opportunities. Crew postings we reviewed were on-site hourly roles, listed as part-time or as full-time or part-time, with availability needed for weekends and holidays.

Do I need experience to work at Netflix House?

The crew postings we reviewed ask for experience in fast-paced retail, hospitality, attractions or food and beverage operations, plus knowledge of Netflix shows and strong customer service skills.

How do I apply for Netflix House jobs?

Apply through Netflix House's official careers site, jobs.netflixhouse.com, which links to current openings by location. Postings say they stay open for at least 7 days and are removed when filled.
