TikTok Remote Jobs: What's Real and How to Avoid Scams

Looking for TikTok remote jobs or a way to work from home for TikTok? Most TikTok jobs aren't fully remote. TikTok's own careers FAQ sets a minimum number of office days, and many "TikTok job" offers sent by text or chat are scams. This guide explains what TikTok's policy says, where content moderator and other TikTok-related jobs come from, how to apply through official channels, and how to spot fake TikTok job offers.

Does TikTok hire remote workers?

TikTok's careers FAQ says employees are required to work in the office a minimum of three days per week, unless restricted by local laws or regulations, and teams can set three to five in-office days based on business needs[1]. TikTok lists offices in cities including Los Angeles and New York[1].

So most TikTok jobs are hybrid or in-office, and many are corporate, technical or business roles. Search TikTok's careers site[3] and check each posting's location before you apply.

TikTok content moderator jobs

"TikTok content moderator jobs" is one of the most popular TikTok job searches. Two things to know:

  • Some roles are posted by TikTok. Search TikTok's careers site for trust and safety or moderation roles, and check the location and requirements[3].
  • Some work goes through vendors. TikTok's FAQ says it typically hires third-party associates through contracted vendors[1]. If a staffing or outsourcing company advertises TikTok-related work, you'd be applying to that company, and the application won't appear in TikTok's own "My applications"[1].

Research any vendor before sharing personal details. The FTC says honest employers will never ask you to pay to get a job[7].

TikTok job scams to watch for

TikTok has warned about scammers who create group chats or send unsolicited messages offering paid tasks, such as following, liking or commenting on videos, or TikTok jobs. TikTok says these messages are scams and that it and its partners don't operate that way[4]. TikTok's Safety Center also lists job scams among the most common online scams[5].

The FTC describes the same pattern as a "task scam": an unexpected text or WhatsApp or Telegram message offers online work, pays small amounts at first, then asks you to deposit money to keep going[6]. Red flags:

  • Unexpected job offers by text or chat app. The FTC says real employers won't contact you that way[6].
  • Paying to earn. The FTC says never pay anyone to get paid[6].
  • Easy money for likes, follows or "boosting." Especially with no interview.
  • Recruiters using personal email addresses. See the next section.

Report scammers or group chats using the reporting tools in the app where you got the message[4], and report job scams to the FTC at ReportFraud.ftc.gov[7].

How to verify a TikTok recruiter

  • Check the email domain. TikTok's careers FAQ says legitimate recruiting emails come from @tiktok.com or @bytedance.com addresses[1].
  • Find the posting yourself. Search for the role on TikTok's careers site instead of clicking a link in a message[3].
  • Check "My applications." If you applied on TikTok's site, your status appears there[1].

How to apply for a job at TikTok

TikTok describes three steps[2]:

  1. Apply. Find a role on TikTok's careers site and upload your resume. TikTok says it doesn't accept email applications[1].
  2. Interview. A series of phone, video or onsite interviews[2]. TikTok says assessments or tasks are common, usually for technical roles, along with several interview rounds[1].
  3. Offer. If you're selected, the recruiter contacts you.

TikTok says it can't give individual status updates because of application volume, so check "My applications" on the careers site[2]. For early-career programs, TikTok's FAQ says you may apply for up to two positions within the application time frame[1]. On interview dress, TikTok says it cares more about your ability to do the job than what you wear[1].

TikTok remote jobs FAQ

Does TikTok hire remote workers?

TikTok's careers FAQ says employees are required to work in the office at least three days per week unless local laws or regulations restrict it, and teams can set three to five office days. Fully remote TikTok roles are uncommon, so check each posting's location.

Are TikTok content moderator jobs remote?

It depends on the role and employer. TikTok's FAQ says it typically hires third-party associates through contracted vendors, so some TikTok-related work is advertised by other companies. Check whether a job is posted on TikTok's careers site or by a vendor, and read its location and in-office requirements.

Is the TikTok job offer I got by text real?

Probably not. TikTok says it and its partners don't recruit by sending unsolicited messages offering paid tasks or jobs, and the FTC says real employers won't contact you that way. TikTok's careers FAQ says genuine recruiting emails come from @tiktok.com or @bytedance.com addresses.

How do I apply for a job at TikTok?

Apply through TikTok's official careers site. TikTok's hiring steps are apply, interview (a series of phone, video or onsite interviews) and offer. TikTok says it doesn't accept email applications.

How do I check my TikTok application status?

Log in to TikTok's careers site and check "My applications." TikTok says it can't give individual status updates because of application volume, and applications made through third-party job platforms won't appear there.

Can I get paid to like or follow TikTok videos?

Offers to pay you for liking, following or commenting on videos are a common task scam. TikTok says such messages are scams and that it and its partners don't operate this way.
