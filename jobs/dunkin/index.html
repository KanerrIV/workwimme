How Dunkin' hiring works

Most Dunkin' restaurants are independently owned and operated by franchisees. Per Dunkin', if you apply to a franchise location, the franchisee, not Dunkin' Brands, would be your only employer and sets its own wages and benefits[1].

Types of Dunkin' jobs

Dunkin' says opportunities at franchise restaurants typically include Crew, Shift Leader and Assistant Manager positions, plus management roles[1]. Corporate roles are listed separately by Dunkin' Brands[3].

Pay and benefits: franchisees set their own wages and benefits, which vary by location[1]. Check the individual posting.

Hiring age

Dunkin' does not publish a single minimum hiring age; requirements depend on the franchisee and local law. Check each posting.

How to apply at Dunkin'

  1. Search openings on careers.dunkindonuts.com by location[2].
  2. Open the posting for the restaurant you want and select Apply.
  3. Your information goes to that franchisee, who handles hiring.

Tips

  • Note shift times in the posting (many list early-morning opener shifts).
  • Apply only through official listings; never pay to apply.

Dunkin' jobs FAQ

How do I apply for Dunkin' jobs?

Search and apply on Dunkin's official careers site, careers.dunkindonuts.com.

Who is my employer at a Dunkin' restaurant?

Per Dunkin', at a franchise location the independent franchisee would be your only employer.

How much do Dunkin' jobs pay?

Franchisees set their own wages, so pay varies by location and role. Check the posting.
