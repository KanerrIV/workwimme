Lululemon Jobs: Educator Roles, Hiring Age and How to Apply

Want to work at Lululemon? Most entry-level Lululemon store jobs are Educator roles, Lululemon's name for its store sales and guest service team. This guide explains what Educators do, the requirements in Lululemon's postings (including the hiring age), the types of schedules on offer, and how to apply through Lululemon's official careers site. It's an independent guide based on Lululemon's own postings.

Types of Lululemon jobs

Lululemon's careers site lets you search openings by business unit: Store, Distribution Centres (DC), Store Support Centre (SSC) and Guest Education Centre (GEC)[1]. For most job seekers, stores are the main entry point. Store listings on the site include:

  • Educator: the core store role, focused on guest experience and product education.
  • Product Operations Educator: a store role listed separately on the careers site.
  • Key Leader, Assistant Manager and Store Manager: store leadership roles.

Store Support Centre roles are head-office jobs, and Distribution Centre and Guest Education Centre roles are listed under their own business units, so filter for the type of work you want[1]. Lululemon's corporate careers page links to the same job search[4].

What a Lululemon Educator does

Lululemon calls the Educator role "the foundation of our success" and describes Educators as experts in world-class guest experience. The Educator postings we reviewed list responsibilities such as[2]:

  • Engaging with guests, assessing their needs and offering purchase and return solutions.
  • Giving technical product education about the value and benefits of products.
  • Handling transactions and omnichannel programs such as buy online, pick up in store, phone sales and ship-from-store orders.
  • Product presentation tasks, such as restocking the floor from inventory and putting items back in place.
  • Cleaning tasks during the shift and following safety policies.
  • Contributing to a respectful, inclusive team.

The postings also list the values Lululemon looks for: inclusion and diversity, integrity, guest experience, personal responsibility, self-awareness, collaboration and enthusiasm[2]. Those values come up again in interviews; our Lululemon interview questions guide covers how to prepare.

Lululemon hiring age and requirements

The U.S. Educator postings we reviewed say applicants must be 18 years of age or older and legally authorized to work in the country where the store is located[2][3]. They also ask that you:

  • Can travel to your assigned store with your own transportation.
  • Are willing to work a flexible schedule, including evenings, weekends and holidays.
  • Are willing to work in an environment with bright lights and loud music, and to move through the store for most of a shift.
  • Are willing to move boxes weighing up to 30 pounds.

A high school diploma, GED or equivalent is listed as a nice-to-have, not a requirement[2]. Requirements can differ by role and country, so read the eligibility section of each posting.

Full-time, part-time and seasonal roles

Lululemon lists Educator roles as full-time, part-time or seasonal. Postings show a time type, such as Full-time for one Reston, Virginia Educator role[2] and Part-time for a seasonal Educator role in Durham, North Carolina[3]. Some seasonal listings are contracts that run through a set date[1], which can suit students or anyone who wants holiday work.

Because postings ask for evening, weekend and holiday availability, be ready to discuss your schedule. For more on balancing hours, see our part-time jobs guide.

Lululemon benefits

The Educator postings we reviewed list benefits including extended health and dental benefits and mental health plans, paid time off, savings and retirement plan matching, an employee discount, fitness and yoga classes, and development courses[2]. The postings note that benefits and perks have eligibility requirements and can change[2].

Pay: pay varies by role and location. Check the individual posting for current details.

How to apply at Lululemon

  1. Search official listings. Go to careers.lululemon.com and filter by country, city and business unit[1].
  2. Pick a store and role. Each posting is tied to a specific store, so read its schedule and eligibility details.
  3. Apply or create a profile. The careers site lets you register a profile with what you're looking for[1].
  4. Watch for contact. Lululemon's postings say only applicants under consideration will be contacted[2].

Tips for getting hired at Lululemon

  • Lead with guest service. The Educator role is built around connecting with guests, so highlight times you helped customers or taught someone something.
  • Know the values. Be ready with examples of collaboration, personal responsibility and enthusiasm.
  • Be honest about availability. Evenings, weekends and holidays are part of most store schedules.
  • Apply only through official channels. The FTC says honest employers will never ask you to pay to get a job[5].

Lululemon jobs FAQ

How old do you have to be to work at Lululemon?

The U.S. Educator postings we reviewed on Lululemon's careers site all said applicants must be 18 years of age or older. Check the eligibility section of the specific posting you apply to.

What is an Educator at Lululemon?

Educator is Lululemon's name for its store sales and guest service role. Lululemon's postings describe Educators as experts in guest experience who connect with guests, share product education and handle transactions, returns and omnichannel orders such as buy online, pick up in store.

Does Lululemon hire part-time?

Yes. Lululemon's careers site lists full-time, part-time and seasonal Educator positions, and some seasonal roles are listed as contracts that run through a set date. Availability varies by store.

How do I apply for a Lululemon job?

Search open positions on Lululemon's official careers site, careers.lululemon.com. You can filter by country, city and business unit, such as Store or Distribution Centre, then apply to the posting. You can also register a profile to save your details.

Do I need retail experience to work at Lululemon?

The Educator postings we reviewed don't list prior retail experience as a requirement. They focus on availability, eligibility and values such as guest experience, collaboration and personal responsibility. A high school diploma or equivalent is listed as nice to have, not required.

How much do Lululemon jobs pay?

Pay varies by role and location. Check the individual posting on Lululemon's official careers site for current details.
