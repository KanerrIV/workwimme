What owners usually want to know first

Is my contract actually exitable? It depends on the contract type, how long you've had it, and the resort or developer involved.
What's the general process look like? Typically starts with a review of your contract and current standing before any next steps are discussed.
What does it involve on my end? Usually just a short conversation about your situation — no paperwork required to simply ask questions.
Outcomes vary by contract and situation, and there's no guaranteed result, timeline, or settlement amount for any individual case. This page is an independent resource to help you understand the general landscape before you decide whether to look into it further.
